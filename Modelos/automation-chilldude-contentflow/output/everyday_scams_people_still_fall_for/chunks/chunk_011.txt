You’re scrolling through Instagram or Facebook, and suddenly, there it is: that designer bag you’ve been eyeing, but for 90% off! Or maybe it’s a brand-new gaming console for half the price. It seems too good to be true, and guess what? It absolutely is. These fake online stores pop up like mushrooms after rain, often using stolen product images, professional-looking websites, and incredibly tempting prices to lure you in. You click, you add to cart, you enter your credit card details, and then... nothing. Your order never arrives, your money is gone, and the website vanishes faster than my motivation on a Monday morning. Sometimes, if you're "lucky," you'll receive a cheap, knock-off version of what you ordered – like getting a potato sack when you expected a designer dress. These scams are particularly prevalent during holiday shopping seasons. Always check reviews, look for legitimate contact information, and be wary of prices that are unbelievably low. If a brand-new iPhone 15 is selling for $100, it’s not a bargain, it’s a trap, plain and simple.
